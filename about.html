Our Story

Serving Brackendowns and the greater Alberton community with flame-grilled traditional meals, fresh biltong, and warm hospitality.

Proudly backed by over 210 positive local reviews and a 4.2-star rating for quality and service.

Our Atmosphere & Community

Designed for every occasion—whether you are dropping in for solo dining, a quick coffee, a family dinner, or watching live sport with a group of friends. We offer a casual, cosy, and trendy environment complete with outdoor seating options and an on-site bar.

Our Mission

To deliver rich, flavorful grilled meals and exceptional table service in an inclusive, fully accessible space for all our guests.

Our Vision

To remain Brackendowns’ top local spot for great food, cold drinks, sports entertainment, and community gatherings.
